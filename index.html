Kitchen Floor Rules

  • Take turns. On your turn every figure can move and then shoot once.
  • Tap a figure to pick it up. Blue tiles show where it can go. Red rings mark who it can hit, with the number you need on the dice.
  • Roll to hit. Any die at or above the number knocks the enemy over.
  • Cover: ducking behind cereal boxes, cans, sponges or spoons makes you harder to hit. Tall things block shots completely.
  • Lying flat soldiers are harder to hit. Bazookas, mortars and flamethrowers don’t care.
  • The Officer gives +1 to friends within 2 tiles.
  • Machine gun & mortar can’t move and fire in the same turn. The mortar lobs over anything, and its blast can topple the neighbors.
  • Knock over the whole enemy army to win!
